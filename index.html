THE SONIC SHIELD

CNNIBLD is a boundary-defying electronic music artist driven by one philosophy: Feed on All Frequencies.

Rejecting the idea that music should fit into a single genre, CNNIBLD crafts high-energy experiences that seamlessly blend hard techno, house, bass, hardstyle, electro, disco, Latin influences, and anything else that ignites a dance floor. Every release is built without limits, every set is designed to surprise, and every performance is a reminder that great music isn't defined by labels…it's defined by emotion and energy.

Known for relentless drops, unexpected transitions, and unforgettable hooks, CNNIBLD delivers performances that keep crowds guessing from the first kick to the final beat. Whether it's underground warehouse energy or a massive festival stage, the mission remains the same: break boundaries, challenge expectations, and unite people through sound.
